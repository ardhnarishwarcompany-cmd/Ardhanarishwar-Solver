# Recruweb Resources Private Limited (Recruweb)

Recruweb Resources Private Limited (Recruweb) is an HR and recruitment services company based in Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India. It provides recruitment, HR outsourcing and career services for both IT and non-IT clients and candidates, and it operates the Ardhnarishwar AI job portal.

## Services for Companies (IT and Non-IT clients)

- **Specialized recruitment**: IT hiring (software developers, data scientists, cybersecurity experts, IT project managers, system administrators) using AI-driven applicant tracking systems; non-IT hiring across finance, healthcare, manufacturing, retail and more (sales executives, financial analysts, healthcare professionals, operations managers).
- **Comprehensive HR outsourcing**: end-to-end HR management (payroll, compliance, employee benefits, performance management) and strategic HR consulting.
- **Talent pipeline development**: building talent pools and succession planning.
- **Employer branding**: brand enhancement and candidate-experience optimization.
- **Flexible workforce solutions**: contract staffing and permanent staffing.
- **Diversity and inclusion**: D&I strategy development and diverse talent sourcing.
- **Performance management and employee development**: customized training programs and performance review systems.

## Services for Candidates (IT and Non-IT)

- **Career coaching and mentorship**: one-on-one career guidance and industry mentors.
- **Exclusive job access**: job market insights (trending skills, salary benchmarks, demand) and access to openings that are not advertised publicly.
- **Skill development and certifications**: workshops, online courses, certifications and technical skill assessments.
- **Resume and interview preparation**: resume optimization and mock interviews with feedback.
- **Job matching and placement**: AI-driven job matching and continuous placement support, including post-placement follow-ups.
- **Relocation and onboarding assistance**: help with moving to a new city and settling into a new role.
- **Contract and freelance opportunities**: flexible work options and gig-economy support.

## Hiring Charge Models for Clients

Recruweb describes three packages for client companies: (1) candidate's annual CTC charges with a 2-month replacement guarantee for a low-package budget; (2) annual CTC charges with a 3-month replacement guarantee for a medium budget; (3) annual CTC charges with a 4-month replacement guarantee for a high budget.
