# Ardhnarishwar — AI-Powered Job Portal by Recruweb

Ardhnarishwar (also spelled Ardhanarishwar) is an AI-powered job portal built and operated by Recruweb Resources Private Limited (Recruweb). In this context the name refers only to Recruweb's AI job portal product. It is not about the Hindu deity Ardhanarishvara, and the assistant must never confuse the two.

- **Ardhnarishwar job portal**: https://ardhnarishwar-job-portal.recruweb.com/
- **Recruweb (parent company) website**: https://recruweb.com/
- **Ardhnarishwar Solver**: the AI assistant of the Ardhnarishwar platform. It helps with career guidance, resume and ATS advice, interview preparation, learning roadmaps, recruitment and business/HR questions, and can also answer general questions.

## What Ardhnarishwar Does

Ardhnarishwar makes job searching smarter through AI-assisted resume scoring, CV building, interview practice and job discovery, so candidates can prepare better and find suitable jobs faster.

## Key Features

- **AI Resume / ATS Scanner**: scans a candidate's resume and scores it against job requirements and ATS (applicant tracking system) criteria, so candidates can improve it.
- **Professional CV Builder**: helps candidates build and format a polished, professional CV/resume.
- **AI Video Interview Practice**: lets candidates practice interviews and receive AI-driven feedback on their answers.
- **Job Discovery Tools**: helps candidates find and browse relevant job opportunities.
- **AI Interview (in Ardhnarishwar Solver)**: voice-based mock interviews with a question-by-question evaluation and a final report.

## Who Is It For?

- **Job seekers and freshers**: improve resumes, practice interviews, discover jobs, plan careers.
- **Professionals changing careers**: get roadmaps, skill-gap guidance and interview preparation.
- **Recruiters and companies**: structured hiring help through Recruweb's recruitment and HR services.
